How it works

Three steps, done.

No apps, no sign-ups, no waiting. Just paste and go.

01
Copy the link

Find any video on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, or other supported platforms and copy its URL from the address bar or share menu.

02
Paste & choose format

Drop the URL into the box above. Pick your preferred format — HD video, standard quality, audio-only MP3, or watermark-free version.

03
Save to device

Hit download and the file lands straight on your device. Works on desktop and mobile without any app installation.
